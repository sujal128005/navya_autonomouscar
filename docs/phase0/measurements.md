# Phase 0 - Vehicle Measurements

## Wheelbase
Wheelbase left side:  74.5 cm
Wheelbase right side: 74.5 cm
(Both sides equal -> chassis square, marks accurate)

## Track width
Track width front: 62.3 cm
Track width rear:  57.65 cm
Note: front wider than rear by ~4.65 cm (custom welded rear axle is narrower).
Combined with low CoG -> caution only if turning added later.

## Rear wheel
Rear wheel: 8-inch BLDC hub motor, 24V 350W, drum brake, SOLID tire
Nominal diameter: 200 mm
Radius: 0.10 m (solid tire, no load compression)
Two motors -> ~700W total drive
Axle: 35 mm shoulder, M14x1.5 threaded end

## Mass and CoG
Total mass: 30-32 kg (heavy - welded steel rear axle + 2x hub motors)
CoG: LOW and REAR-BIASED. Most mass low at rear over welded steel + hub motors.
  Low CoG = good stability. Rear bias = good drive traction, light front (fine).
Implication: heavy car -> drive slow, trigger stop early for coasting/momentum.

## Hand-push straight test (motors off)
Result: rolls straight, no drift. Front wheel lock validated.
